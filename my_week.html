Copilot reads your inbox and calendar. This file remembers. Nothing you paste here leaves your browser.
You are looking at sample data (invented names and tasks, so you can see how it works). It disappears the moment you paste your own list.

1Ask Copilot (work mode)

Go through my emails and Teams messages from the last 7 days. List every action item that someone is expecting from ME (not things I asked others). Include implicit asks ("would be great if you could..."). Return ONLY a table, no commentary, with columns: Task | Requested by | Due date (YYYY-MM-DD or blank) | Source (email subject) Be specific: "Send comments on the PAD draft to Maria", not "follow up".
Then copy Copilot's table.

2Paste Copilot's answer

Already-listed items are skipped.
Prefer Microsoft To Do? After Copilot gives you the table, reply to it: "Add each of these as a task in my Microsoft To Do, with the due dates." Copilot creates them directly, and you skip this tool for that tab. Use this page when you want the three tabs in one place, the Nudge button, or a list that Copilot can't hold (suggestion from Kamila Anna Galeza, thank you).
