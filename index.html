Three tools for the pains
you feel every week.

Ready to use, no install, no account, no IT ticket. Each one is a single web file: open it in any browser, on any device. Nothing you type leaves your computer. Where a tool needs reading or thinking (your inbox, your calendar, quiz questions), it hands you a prompt for Copilot and takes the answer back.

My Week

The pain: 140 unread emails and no idea what you owe, who owes you, and what you must prepare.

Three tabs. I owe: everything people expect from you, sorted overdue / today / this week. Waiting on others: what you asked and never got back, with a one-click Nudge that opens a polite reminder in Outlook. Meeting prep: what each meeting this week needs from you, due the day before. Copilot reads; the file remembers.

Meeting Time Finder

The pain: "Does 9am work?" Nine emails later, nobody knows whose 9am.

Pick the cities on the call (DC, Lima, Delhi, Nairobi, 90 to choose from). One glance shows where everyone is inside working hours, with daylight saving handled. It proposes the three best slots and copies a ready-to-paste line with every city's local time for the invite.

Workshop Quiz

The pain: the post-lunch slot of every training, when the room goes quiet and the phones come out.

A live quiz on the projector. Big text, 30-second timer, reveal, team scoreboard, podium. No accounts, no wifi, no app for participants. Copy the built-in prompt, and Copilot writes the questions about your topic in the right format.

How to keep a tool

  1. Right-click the button above and choose "Save link as". Put the file on your desktop or in OneDrive.
  2. Double-click it whenever you need it. It opens in your browser and remembers what you typed last time (on that computer).
  3. Send the file to a colleague like any attachment. It works for them too, empty and ready.

Want it different? Ask, don't code

You don't need to know how these files work. Drag the file into Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini or Copilot and say what you want in plain English. It returns a new file. Examples that work:

  1. Make it Spanish
  2. In My Week, add a fourth tab called "Promised to counterparts"
  3. In the time finder, add a whole-week view and exclude Friday afternoons
  4. In the quiz, play a sound when the timer hits zero and show the fun fact in bigger text
  5. Make it work as an icon on my phone's home screen

Want a tool that doesn't exist yet? Describe it the same way: Build a single self-contained HTML file that ... Inputs are ... It should show ... Save everything in the browser. Works on a phone.

Golden rule: the tool files contain no data, so they're safe to share and to remix in any chatbot. Your real data goes in after, in your own browser. Never paste internal or "Official Use Only" content into a non-Bank chatbot: for anything about your inbox, calendar or projects, the prompt goes to Copilot.
