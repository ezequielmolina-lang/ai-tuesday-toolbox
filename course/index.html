Five mornings

There is a report you really wanted to read. You downloaded it. It is 380 pages, and it is November. From this week you do not have to find the time: three minutes a morning, for five mornings, and the report comes to you.

3 November 2026 • The AI Tuesday Toolbox

Everyone has one. The flagship of your sector. The evaluation that everyone quotes. The strategy you were cc'd on. The book a colleague swore would change how you work. You saved it for the flight, and on the flight you slept. Not a failing; the week is the week.

So we asked AI to do the one thing it is genuinely good at with a long document: not summarize it, but turn it into something you will actually finish. One short lesson a day. A question you answer before you read, because guessing first is how things stick. One verbatim quote with the page number, so you can check the AI did not make it up. One two-minute thing to do with it. We call it a reverse course: you do not go to it, it comes to you, at breakfast, out of your own download folder.

This edition is one small app and two finished courses inside it. The third is yours.

The WDR 2026 in five mornings

The World Development Report 2026, The Promise of Artificial Intelligence, came out on 4 August. It is the Bank's own argument about the technology this newsletter is about, and most of us have read the press release. We gave the full PDF to an AI with one instruction: five mornings, teach the actual content, quote the page. Then we checked every quote against the text of the PDF at the page it cited. All five were there. Open the page and read the sentence anyway; that is the habit this edition is really about.

2 · Make

Now do it with yours

The same page turns any document into five mornings. The prompt is written; you add the file. Internal documents go to Copilot (Cowork or Chat, file attached). Public ones work in Copilot, ChatGPT or Claude. If you have to ask whether a document is public, it is not.

Open "Make one" and copy the prompt. Type what the document is and who the course is for, or leave both blank.

Attach the document and paste the prompt. The prompt tells the AI to stop rather than write from memory if it cannot read the file, and to answer in the chat, not as a Word document. It will, eventually.

Paste what comes back. A block of text that starts with a curly bracket. It is the one block of text in this edition you are allowed not to read. The page checks it and opens Morning 1. If the answer was cut off, the page says so and gives you the message to send back.

Make it on your laptop, take it on your phone. To share a course, download the file from the course page and send it; colleagues load it on the same page. No accounts, no server: the course is a file, and the page reads it on your device. First time? The Make page has three public PDFs to try before you use your own.

3 · Read

AI and work: the evidence, in five mornings

The second finished course is for the question you get at dinner, "so is AI taking the jobs?", and for the meeting where someone quotes a LinkedIn post. Five mornings, each built from the source itself, with the link in the lesson and every number checked against it.

  • Morning 1: Canaries in the Coal Mine. Brynjolfsson, Chandar and Chen on US payroll data: no economy-wide displacement, but 22 to 25 year olds in AI-exposed jobs are 19 percent below where they would be had they kept pace, through hiring, not layoffs. The 13 percent you have seen quoted is the 2025 regression estimate; the August 2026 revision uses a simpler gap, 15 percent then, 19 now.
  • Morning 2: the jagged frontier. 758 BCG consultants: inside the frontier, 12.2 percent more tasks and 25.1 percent faster; on the one task outside it, 19 percent less likely to be right.
  • Morning 3: the cybernetic teammate. 776 Procter and Gamble professionals: one person with AI matched a pair without it, and the silos between R&D and Commercial dissolved.
  • Morning 4: in the classroom. Two randomized trials from the EvidencIA base: in Ankara, open ChatGPT during practice meant 48 percent better practice and a 17 percent worse exam; in Benin City, Copilot as a guided tutor with teachers in the room raised English scores by 0.24 standard deviations in six weeks. Same technology. The design did the work.
  • Morning 5: Ethan Mollick. Co-author of Mornings 2 and 3, the four rules of Co-Intelligence, his February guide to the agentic era, and Co-Existence, out on 20 October.

Reality check

  • The AI will sometimes cite a page that says something slightly different. That is why the page is there. A wrong page is a finding, not a failure, and the fastest way to learn whether your AI read the file or only the first twenty pages.
  • Three minutes is the design. If the AI returns 600-word lessons, you have a document again. Ask for shorter.
  • Internal documents stay inside the Bank's tools. The public chatbots are for public PDFs.
  • Courses live on the device where you built them. The file is the course; keep it in the folder next to the thing it teaches.

Five mornings, fifteen minutes, one report you can finally say you have read.

Which is more than most of us could say about the WDR last week.
