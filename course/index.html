Five
mornings

The report you downloaded in August. Three minutes a morning, on your phone. Five mornings and you have read it.

Everyone has one. The flagship of your sector, the evaluation everyone quotes, the strategy you were cc'd on. You saved it for the flight; on the flight you slept.

So we asked AI to turn a long document into something you will actually finish. Each morning: one question before you read, because guessing first is how it sticks; a three-minute lesson; one verbatim quote with its page, so you can check the AI did not make it up; and one thing to do today.
