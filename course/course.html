Turn any document into five mornings

The flagship of your sector, the evaluation everyone quotes, the strategy you were cc'd on, the 300-page book you saved for the flight. The AI reads it; you get one three-minute lesson a day with a guess-first question and a page reference you can check. Make it on your laptop, take it on your phone. Two steps.

Give the prompt and the document to your AI

Internal document (anything from WBDocs, a draft, an internal review): Copilot Cowork or Copilot Chat, with the file attached as a PDF, not Word, so the page numbers mean something (Copilot usually needs the file in your OneDrive first). Internal documents never go to a public chatbot. If you have to ask whether a document is public, it is not.
Public document (a published report, a WDR, a disclosed project document, a book you own): any of them works. Attach the PDF; a link alone is often not read in full.

First time? Try it with a public PDF so the first success is one paste away:

Copilot has no link that opens with a question: copy, open Copilot, attach the file, paste. The prompt asks for a specific format that this page can read, and tells the AI to stop rather than write from memory if it cannot read the file.

Paste what the AI returned

It will answer with a block of text that starts with {. It is the one block of text in this edition you are allowed not to read. Copy all of it and paste it here; the page checks it and builds the course.

Check before you trust. The page references are the point: open the document at the page the lesson cites and read the sentence. If it is not there, the AI invented it, and you have learned one thing about the document and one about the AI. A wrong page is a finding, not a failure.
